Third-party material in this repository

The MIT license in LICENSE covers the code. It does not cover the dataset
material below, which keeps its own terms. The only source pixels included are
the 64 ChestX-ray14 radiographs, and no rendered images are included. The
answers in data/answers.csv were produced by the models named in
data/plan.json.

BBBC039v1, Broad Bioimage Benchmark Collection
  License: CC0 1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
  Source: https://bbbc.broadinstitute.org/BBBC039/
  Included: image identifiers, image sizes, the number of pixels above 255 in
  each image, and nucleus counts derived from the ground-truth masks, in
  data/items/bbbc200.csv.
  Cite: Caicedo et al., BBBC039v1: Nuclei of U2OS cells in a chemical screen
  (2018); Ljosa et al., Nature Methods 9:637 (2012); Caicedo et al.,
  Cytometry A 95(9):952-965 (2019).

SLAKE, Liu et al., ISBI 2021
  License of the annotations: CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/BoKelvin/SLAKE (test split, English
  closed questions); project page https://www.med-vqa.com/slake/
  Included: 50 questions with their answers and modality labels, in
  data/items/slake50.csv. Changes: a 50-question subset was selected, and the
  prompts in data/plan.json append a reply format to each question.
  Not included: SLAKE images, which come from upstream collections with their
  own terms.
  Cite: Liu et al., SLAKE: A semantically-labeled knowledge-enhanced dataset
  for medical visual question answering, ISBI 2021, 1650-1654.

NIH ChestX-ray14, NIH Clinical Center
  Terms: no restriction on use; NIH requires a link to the original download,
  citation of Wang et al. (2017) and acknowledgment of the NIH Clinical Center
  as the data provider.
  Source: https://nihcc.app.box.com/v/ChestXray-NIHCC, read through the
  Hugging Face mirror BahaaEldin0/NIH-Chest-Xray-14
  Included: the 64 radiographs used, as PNG files in data/raw/chestxray14/images,
  with local image identifiers, NIH image file names and yes/no answers derived
  from the NIH labels in data/items/chest64.csv. The questions were written for
  this study. The opacity questions use the NIH Effusion label.
  Cite: Wang et al., ChestX-ray8: Hospital-scale chest X-ray database and
  benchmarks on weakly-supervised classification and localization of common
  thorax diseases, CVPR 2017, 3462-3471.
